[about]

Hi, I'm Chloe.

I'm a UX/UI and product designer in Waterloo, Ontario. I like being the person who connects design and development: I design something, then I open my code editor and build it.

Right now I'm the Web and Digital Marketing Coordinator at The Food Bank of Waterloo Region. I design and update web pages, build email campaigns, check my pages against AODA and WCAG, draw original illustrations, and report on how the site is doing.

What I care about

  • Never in your way

    Guest checkout, optional quizzes, a skip button when you need one. People shouldn't have to jump through hoops to get what they came for.

  • Built for groups

    Board game tables, group trips, quiz results you share with friends. My favourite problems involve more than one person deciding something.

  • Accessible from the start

    I check my pages against AODA and WCAG as I go, because fixing it at the end is harder for everyone.
